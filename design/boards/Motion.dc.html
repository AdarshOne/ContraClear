Motion spec

Six named animations. Each answers one question, each can be skipped, each has a reduced-motion version. Nothing decorative.
Name
Question
Behaviour
1 Trace zoom
Where did this come from?
The contract jumps to the quote with no travel; a soft ring shrinks onto it in 450 ms. Reduced motion: highlight only.
2 Formula build-up
How was this date worked out?
Each line of the working appears 180 ms after the last. Reduced motion: all lines at once.
3 Staged processing
What is the tool doing?
Six real stages. Pages turn into clause blocks; values move into Money, Dates and Duties trays, which become workspace sections. Under 8 s, skippable. Reduced motion: the stage list only.
4 Confirm and edit
What changed?
The card leaves Needs your check and the count updates; focus moves to the next item. Edits add a line to History. Reduced motion: instant.
5 Calendar preview
What will happen?
Events appear one by one, 80 ms apart, so you can read what will be created. Reduced motion: all at once.
6 Lock and network badge
Is my data safe?
The lock icon settles in 500 ms on lock and unlock. The badge count is underlined once when it changes. Reduced motion: no movement; the text still updates.
