Style guide and components

ContraClear, final. Less, but better.

Rules

Principles
Ma: about 60% empty, contract column 640 px, 1 px dividers. Kanso: two states, one primary button, one menu level. Shibui: black, white, greys, no decoration. Omotenashi: likely answer first, agreed items pre-confirmed, shortcuts and next steps always offered.
Colour
Unchanged. #111, #333, #444, #6B6B6B, #BDBDBD, #E3E3E3, #F4F4F4, #FFF. Status only: green #1E7B3A on #EAF5EE, yellow #A66F00 on #FFF4D6, red #C62828 on #FCE9E8.
Type
Inter 14 to 32 for the interface (14 only for labels next to 16 px text). Source Serif 4 at 18 px for contract wording, so original text always looks different from plain English.
Space
4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Between ideas: 48 or more.
Layout
Library 272 (64 collapsed), What needs you 440 (500 at 1920, 360 at 1024). Under 900: Library, then Contract and items, then the preview as a full-screen sheet.
Keys
Arrows move · Enter confirms · 1 or 2 picks a side · E edits · Shift+Enter confirms all agreed · C calendar · Cmd/Ctrl+K everything · Esc closes.
Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA. Black text on every tint is 16 to 1 or better; status icons are at least 3.9 to 1. 44 px targets. Visible 3 px focus. Live regions for the badge, toasts and stages. Works at 200% zoom by switching to stacked panes.
Voice
Grade 8. Say what happened, then what to do. Say what was not found. Never sound sure when the checks disagree. Trust and consent copy is word for word the same on desktop and mobile.
Never shown
Pricing, plans, seats, roles, tours, workflow builders, templates, e-sign, redlines, chat, settings beyond the seven listed.

Component inventory

Two main states
Drop (whole window) and Workspace (library, preview, What needs you, 90-day strip). Panels: side sheet 520 px, centred panel 600 px, popover 420 px. Under 760 px every panel becomes a bottom sheet.
Status chip
Both checks agree Checks disagree: please confirm Couldn’t verify
Only use of colour. Always icon and words.
Item card
Status, page link, plain sentence, who, amount, due, quote; conflict view side by side; How was this worked out?; Confirm, Edit, This is wrong, Dismiss; History.
Collapsed line
“12 confirmed by both checks”, “Not found in this contract”. Opens in place.
Track menu
Floats under selected text: Date, Amount, Duty, then title, who owes, reminder. Tracked text gets a dashed outline.
Library tree and list
Next 30 days, Portfolio, All, by counterparty, type, year and source. Columns: name, counterparty, type, next deadline, status. Latest badge for amendments.
Preview toolbar
Page up and down, page count, zoom, thumbnails, find, print, download original. Page map on the right edge.
Command bar
Search across contracts (keyword and meaning), filter chips, recent searches, all commands with their keys.
90-day strip
Ticks with date and amount; hover for the breakdown; click focuses the item.
Network badge
Bottom right on every screen: what left the device and how many bytes.
Not legal advice note
Small info line next to calendar creation, computed deadlines and totals.
Buttons
One black primary per screen. Outline secondary. Quiet text buttons for dismissive actions. 44 px minimum.
Key cap
Shown beside its action, never alone.
Prototype chrome
Dashed bar on flow boards. Not part of the product.
