Limits of this prototype

Fixed-width boards
Every board reflows from 360 to 1920. 1920 and 1024 boards exist for the Workspace; flow boards are 1440 and 390. Resize any board to check other widths.
Placeholder data
Only the Brightwell contract has full text. Other contracts show a toast saying so.
Google steps
Shown as neutral placeholders. The real consent and picker screens are Google’s.
To confirm in build
Model size (380 MB), page limit (300), magic-link expiry (15 min), and that fonts are self-hosted so the badge can honestly read 0.
