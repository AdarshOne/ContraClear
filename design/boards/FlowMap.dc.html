How to use the prototypes

Flow boards
The dashed bar at the top is prototype chrome, not product UI. It steps through the screens in order and switches each screen’s states. Buttons inside the screens are linked too.
Workspace
Fully interactive: arrows, Enter, E, 1 and 2, Shift+Enter, C, Cmd/Ctrl+K, Esc. Select text in the contract to track it. Drag a library row onto a counterparty.
Widths
Every board is fluid from 360 to 1920. Fixed-width boards: Workspace at 1440, 1920, 1024 and 390, and a 390 version of every flow board (names ending in M).
Google screens
Google’s own consent and picker windows are shown as neutral placeholders, not copies of Google’s interface.
